Our Story

How We Met

Our story began back in college, where we first met within the same circle of friends. At the time, we were not particularly close, but we would casually talk and see each other during gatherings and school activities. We were simply friends then, sharing simple conversations and familiar moments within our group. Looking back, those ordinary college days were quietly laying the foundation for the beautiful journey we share today.

Our First Date

Although we had been casually talking as friends from time to time, everything began to change in June 2020. What started as a simple "Kamusta?" gradually turned into daily conversations. Those light exchanges soon became something we both looked forward to every day.

By December 2020, we decided to watch a movie together and share a meal at Cabalen in SM Dasmariñas. What seemed like a simple plan became our first official date. From then on, movie nights and lunch outings became more frequent, and we found ourselves spending more and more time together.

As the days passed, our connection naturally grew stronger. Then on April 18, 2022, what began as frequent dates and meaningful conversations finally became official — we decided to take the next step and begin our relationship.

Memorable Moments

Over the years, we have shared many meaningful moments together, each becoming a treasured part of our journey. Whenever we travel, we are drawn to places that let us explore and enjoy more — from the peaceful beauty of nature and relaxing beaches to inspiring museums, scenic parks, and even lively malls where we simply enjoy walking side by side. No matter where we go, what matters most is being together and creating memories we will always cherish.

FAQ

What time should I arrive? +

Kindly arrive 30 minutes to 1 hour before the ceremony begins at 9:00 AM. This will allow ample time for parking and seating. Please also consider traffic in Tagaytay.

Is there parking available? +

Yes, parking will be available for both the ceremony and reception at the venue.

May I bring a plus one? +

Due to limited seating, we kindly ask that you inform us in advance if you plan to bring a plus one. Our seating arrangements are carefully organized and allotted based on the guests who have already registered through our RSVP. Letting us know ahead of time will help us properly coordinate and adjust the seating accordingly. Thank you for your understanding.

Until when can I register for RSVP? +

RSVP will close 10 days before the wedding and will be disabled on this site. This will allow us enough time to finalize the guest list and arrange seating with the venue. We will also send a private message to each family and friend for final confirmation before submitting the final guest list to our suppliers and event coordinators.

Will the reception be held indoors? +

Yes. The reception will be held in an air-conditioned indoor venue for the comfort of our guests.

What's the best way to contact you? +

For any questions or clarifications, please feel free to reach out to us through our Facebook accounts. We will respond as soon as possible, and we kindly ask for up to 24 hours for a reply. Thank you so much.
